Privacy Policy for ScanPRO AI

Effective Date: 16 December 2025

ScanPRO AI (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an individually owned application developed and operated by Sumit Kumar. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, notify, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (“App”), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide, improve, and secure our services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and contact details if voluntarily provided.
  • Device Data: Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, IDFA), operating system, and model.
  • Usage and Analytics Data: App activity, feature usage, and session analytics via Google Analytics and Firebase.
  • Advertising Data: Through Google AdMob for displaying ads to free users.
  • Crash and Diagnostics Data: Automatically collected for app performance improvements.
  • Payment Data: Processed securely via Google Play and Apple App Store. We do not handle or store payment credentials directly.
  • Scanned and Uploaded Content: Documents, images, or files scanned or uploaded by you, processed using AI and OCR for recognition features. Content may be processed locally or securely on cloud servers.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To operate and maintain the app’s core functions
  • To enhance app performance and AI recognition accuracy
  • To provide relevant ads to free users
  • To offer premium features and manage subscriptions
  • To analyze app usage and trends
  • To detect, prevent, or address technical issues and abuse
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Advertising

ScanPRO AI uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Ads may be personalized based on your interests or non-personalized depending on your privacy settings. Users can manage ad preferences or withdraw consent at any time via:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Premium features and subscriptions are processed exclusively through Google Play and Apple App Store billing. We do not store, access, or process any financial or payment information directly. All transactions comply with the store’s respective privacy and security policies.

5. Third-Party Services Used

We integrate with third-party tools for analytics, AI features, advertising, and performance monitoring, including:

  • Google AdMob (Ads)
  • Google Analytics / Firebase (Analytics)
  • Crashlytics (Error reporting)
  • Firebase Storage (Cloud file handling)
  • OpenAI API / OCR Models (AI-powered scanning and text extraction)

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide app functionality. Information may be deleted when:

  • You delete your account or app data
  • The data is no longer required for operational or legal reasons

7. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data, including:

  • Encryption (HTTPS / SSL) for all communications
  • Secure server environments and restricted data access
  • Routine security reviews and updates
  • Compliance with Google Play’s data safety requirements

8. AI and OCR Processing

ScanPRO AI employs artificial intelligence and OCR for scanning, reading, and text recognition. Depending on your settings, content may be processed:

  • Locally: On your device, without leaving it.
  • Cloud-based: Temporarily uploaded to secure servers for AI analysis, then automatically deleted after processing.

We do not store or reuse any user data for model training or third-party sharing.
